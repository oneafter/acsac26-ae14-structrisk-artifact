Claim 2: Within-project ranking and LLM-LCR
===========================================

Within-project ranking is the paper's primary supported result. On ten positive
multi-finding projects, StructRisk+LLM-LCR reaches NDCG@5/MAP = 0.9023/0.8750
versus 0.5126/0.4696 for Public-Severity. The difference is significant against
Public-Severity (p = 0.0049 for NDCG@5 and p = 0.0068 for MAP). LLM-LCR changes
only a small number of same-project score blocks.
