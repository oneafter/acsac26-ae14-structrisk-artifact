License and Data Notes

The StructRisk research artifact scripts are released under the MIT License.
Derived benchmark records, cached outputs, and third-party data remain subject
to their original licenses and usage terms.

This public package is provided for ACSAC 2026 artifact evaluation and
research reproducibility.

StructRisk scripts in artifact/StructRisk/scripts/ are research artifact code for
reproduction use. The package also includes derived benchmark records, ranked outputs,
summary tables, cached LLM prompts/responses, and comparator logs used by the
submitted paper.

The package does not include full third-party source mirrors, raw fuzzing
campaigns, original third-party corpora, or public PoC archives. MAGMA-related
files are processed inventory and crash-backed evidence-card slices used for the
paper's external validation.

CVE identifiers, NVD/CNA status, public severity fields, public CNA/NVD
contact emails, and public issue/advisory links provide provenance for auditing
labels. LLM inputs exclude identifying and outcome metadata to prevent
evaluation leakage.
